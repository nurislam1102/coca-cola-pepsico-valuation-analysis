# Analytical Findings: The Valuation Gap (KO vs PEP)

Data: SEC 10-K filings FY2021-FY2025; prices = closing prices on **2025-12-31**. Student project, public data, not investment advice.

## Answers to the six research questions
1. **Faster revenue growth (FY2021 to FY2025, 4 intervals):** Coca-Cola. Revenue CAGR **5.53%** vs PepsiCo **4.27%** (gap 1.26 percentage points). PepsiCo is the larger company: FY2025 revenue $93.9B vs $47.9B (1.96x). Both grew revenue in every year; growth slowed for both (KO 17.1% in FY2021 to 1.9% in FY2025; PEP 12.9% to 2.3%).
2. **Stronger margins:** Coca-Cola, clearly. Five-year average operating margin **25.3%** vs **13.3%** (+12.0 pp); average net margin **24.2%** vs **9.8%** (+14.4 pp). FY2025: operating margin 28.7% vs 12.2%; net margin 27.3% vs 8.8%.
3. **Did revenue growth consistently become earnings growth?** No. Revenue rose in all 5 years for both, but net income rose in the same year in only **3 of 5** years for KO and **4 of 5** for PEP. KO net income fell in FY2022 (-2.3%) and FY2024 (-0.8%); PEP's fell in FY2025 (-14.0%).
4. **Higher valuation multiple on the selected date (2025-12-31):** PepsiCo, narrowly. GAAP P/E **23.9x** vs Coca-Cola **23.0x**: a 0.9x gap, PEP at a 4.0% premium.
5. **What could help explain a valuation gap:** the data show Coca-Cola with much higher margins and faster revenue growth but a P/E that is *not* higher than PepsiCo's on this date, and the sign of the P/E gap flipped across year-ends (2021: +5.3x, 2022: -0.9x, 2023: +2.0x, 2024: -3.4x, 2025: +0.9x for PEP minus KO). So in this sample, margins and growth alone do **not** line up with the P/E ranking. Possible, untested interpretations: PEP's depressed FY2025 GAAP EPS inflating its P/E; differing expectations about future earnings; business mix; dividend/balance-sheet differences.
6. **What cannot be concluded:** why investors paid these prices; whether either stock was over- or under-valued; anything about future returns. P/E uses GAAP EPS that includes one-off items (e.g. KO 'Other operating charges' of $1,261M/FY2025 and $4,163M/FY2024 per its 10-K). EV/EBITDA and debt-based metrics were excluded (see Limitations). One valuation date is a single snapshot.

## Three dashboard takeaways (observation, then interpretation)
1. **Margins, not growth, separate the two businesses.** Observation: KO's average operating margin is 25.3% vs PEP's 13.3%, while revenue CAGR differs by only 1.3 pp. Interpretation (untested): different business mixes (beverage concentrate vs beverages plus snacks).
2. **Revenue growth did not reliably turn into profit growth.** Observation: net income and revenue rose together in 3/5 (KO) and 4/5 (PEP) years; FY2021-FY2025 net income CAGR 7.6% (KO) vs 2.0% (PEP).
3. **The 'valuation gap' is small and unstable at this date.** Observation: on 2025-12-31 PEP's GAAP P/E was 23.9x vs KO's 23.0x (4.0% premium), and the gap changed sign across the five year-ends. Caution: one snapshot, GAAP EPS only.
